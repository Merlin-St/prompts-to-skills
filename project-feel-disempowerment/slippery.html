The Boiling Frog

You just experienced skill decay in under 2 minutes. In real life, this happens over months and years with GPS (navigation), calculators (mental math), spell-check (spelling), and autocomplete (memory). Each delegation is small and reasonable. The aggregate effect is not.

What Comes Next?

AI beat you at a simple pattern game. But it's also beating humans at chess, Go, protein folding, legal research, medical diagnosis, and creative writing. The question isn't whether AI will be better—it's which skills you want to keep.
